# Theme credits

Inky's ready-made themes come in two kinds. The ones below are colour palettes
from open-source projects, adapted to Inky's roles (page, text, accent, sidebar,
border and a few finer colours). Each project's licence was checked in its own
repository, and its copyright notice is reproduced below as the licences
require.

Where "adapted" is noted, a colour was changed slightly so text, links and
sidebar text keep a contrast of at least 4.5:1. The other ready-made themes
(Paper, Sepia, Mint, Fog, Sand, Lavender, Slate, Graphite, Ink, Ember, High
Contrast Light and High Contrast Dark) are Inky originals.

## Adaptations

- **Solarized Light**: text is a slightly darker slate than the palette's
  base01, and links a deeper blue than its blue (adapted).
- **Solarized Dark**: links are a lighter blue than the palette's blue
  (adapted).
- **Catppuccin Latte**: links are a slightly deeper blue (adapted).
- **Tokyo Night Day**: text and links are a slightly deeper blue (adapted).
- **Everforest Light**: links are a deeper blue (adapted).
- The rest use the palettes' own colours for page, text, accent and sidebar.
  Where a palette defines syntax colours, they are mapped onto Inky's six
  syntax roles.
